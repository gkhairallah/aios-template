Use the **vault-ops** skill (Weekly review). Produce the rollup: active and stalled projects,
overdue / due-soon by deadline, decisions logged this week, and fleeting notes worth promoting.

**Include the currency check:** for every `status: active` project, verify `📌 Current Focus` and
the frontmatter still match the latest evidence, and flag stale lines that later entries have
contradicted or resolved (Conventions § ⏳ Snapshots vs Living Notes — write-through misses).
Propose rewrites of the stale living surfaces; never edit the dated blocks.

Offer to act on any item (promote a note, change a status, refresh a Current Focus) with approval.
